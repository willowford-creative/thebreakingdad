Dating as a single parent in the UK can feel like navigating a complex maze. Your time is limited, your responsibilities are non-negotiable, and your priorities have shifted dramatically from when you were dating pre-kids.   
  
But that doesn’t mean finding love again is impossible.   
  
In fact, with the right approach, it can be deeply rewarding.

This guide is designed to offer practical, no-nonsense advice based on real-life experience (albeit, it's been a little while since I went through this process personally!)—because dating as a single mum or dad is different from dating without kids.   
  
We’ll explore the best dating apps for single parents, how to set expectations, and how to balance love and parenting, all while ensuring your child’s wellbeing remains a priority.

## The Reality of Dating for Single Parents in the UK

Let’s be honest—dating as a single parent isn’t like dating in your twenties.   
  
When I first dipped my toe back into the dating pool via apps like Tinder, I quickly realised many people didn’t understand what it means to be a parent first and foremost. Although in the first few conversations, they'd claim they didn't mind that I had a young daughter, but the reality of not coming first when I had my daughter would soon wear thin.

I'll never forget one person telling me that I should just *not* see my then three year old more than once every other weekend if I ever wanted a 'proper' relationship.

Needless to say...I made excuses there and then and left the date.  
  
In fact, through the dating process,some didn’t get why I had to stay in contact with my daughter’s mum, others struggled with the idea that I couldn’t drop everything for a last-minute date. But through trial and error, I found that there are ways to make dating work without compromising my role as a father.  
  
It ultimately came down to crystal clear communication on my profile.

The great thing about dating apps is that you have the opportunity to use user-friendly dating apps to build detailed profiles that clearly communicate your priorities and life situation as a single parent.

Eventually, I met my fiancée on Tinder. The difference? We both had children. Knowing that we shared the same priorities from the outset made things so much easier.

## Preparing for Single Parent Dating

### Identifying Your Dating Purpose and Goals

Before diving into the world of single parent dating, it’s really important to identify your dating purpose and goals. This self-reflection will help you navigate the dating scene with clarity and confidence. Here are some tips to help you prepare:

1. **Reflect on Your Motivations**: Take a bit of time to ask yourself *why* you want to start dating again. Are you looking for a serious relationship, seeking companionship, or simply wanting to meet new people? Being honest with yourself about your motivations will help you make sure your dating journey is positive and fulfilling. **I got this wrong in the early days.** I initially used dating as a way to distract myself from the emotional heartache that came with adjusting to life as a [co-parent](https://thebreakingdad.co.uk/separated-parents/what-is-co-parenting-the-ultimate-guide-to-raising-happy-kids-after-separation/). In retrospect, I regret that I didn't reflect inwardly more before dating. It was only when I began to truly accept my new life as a co-parent, that I was able to really understand my motivation for dating because I knew what I wanted from my life moving forward.
2. **Consider Your Children’s Needs**: As a single parent, your children’s well-being is paramount. I know this sounds obvious, but you'd be amazed at how many times I've seen people get carried away in the excitement of a new relationship. Think about how dating might impact your kids and be prepared to address any challenges that may arise. Ensuring your children feel secure and supported is essential as you embark on this new chapter. Time is on your side here. My partner Rosie and I took our time to introduce each other to our children – gauging the situation specifically for each child.
3. **Think About Your Ideal Partner**: What qualities are you looking for in a potential partner? Do you prefer someone who is also a single parent, or someone who is understanding and supportive of your role as a single parent? Make a list of your non-negotiables and deal-breakers to help you identify like-minded people who align with your values.
4. **Evaluate Your Lifestyle**: Balancing dating with parenting responsibilities can be challenging. Consider how you’ll manage your time and commitments to ensure you can dedicate time to both your children and your dating life. Being realistic about your availability will help you set appropriate expectations with potential dates.
5. **Seek Support**: Make sure you take time to talk to friends, family members, or a therapist about your decision to start dating again. Having a support system can provide valuable advice and encouragement, making your dating journey smoother and more enjoyable. Dating *can* be a challenging process and it doesn't always go smoothly whilst you seek out the one. Having people around you who can dust you off and pick you back up is a massive help.

By taking the time to reflect on your dating purpose and goals, you’ll be better equipped to navigate the world of single parent dating and find a partner who is right for you and your family.

## How to Approach Single Parent Dating Successfully

### **1. Be Honest From the Start**

Don’t hide the fact that you have kids. If a potential match isn’t comfortable with dating someone who has children, it’s better to know upfront. Dating a single mom comes with unique dynamics and challenges. Potential partners need to be flexible and understanding of the limited availability due to parenting responsibilities. It’s important to respect boundaries regarding discipline and take relationships slowly to build trust, as single moms often have distinct priorities and experiences that influence their romantic relationships.

### **2. Prioritise Your Child’s Wellbeing**

Dating should complement your parenting, not compete with it. Being in a relationship with a single mother comes with unique challenges and experiences, such as the need for trust, flexibility, and understanding. Introduce new partners gradually and ensure your child feels secure.

### **3. Set Realistic Expectations**

You probably won’t have the luxury of spontaneous dates, and that’s okay. Focus on quality over quantity—meaningful connections rather than fleeting encounters.

### **4. Join Single Parent Communities**

Connecting with other single parents (through apps like Frolo or local groups) can help with emotional support, friendship, and even potential dating opportunities.

### **5. Don’t Feel Guilty About Dating**

It’s natural to worry about how dating affects your child, but your happiness matters too. A fulfilled parent is a better parent.

## **FAQs: Dating for Single Parents**

### **Is it hard to date as a single parent?**

Dating as a single parent comes with unique challenges, such as limited time, childcare responsibilities, and ensuring a potential partner understands your priorities. However, with the right approach and platforms, it can also be a rewarding and fulfilling experience.

### **When should I introduce my child to someone I’m dating?**

It’s best to wait until the relationship is serious and stable before introducing your child. Every family dynamic is different, but many experts suggest waiting at least six months before involving your child in the relationship. This expert advice helps ensure that the relationship is solid and provides a stable environment for your child.

### **What are the best dating apps for single parents in the UK?**

Some of the best dating apps for single parents include Frolo Dating, [Match.com](http://Match.com), eHarmony, Tinder, Bumble, and Single Parent Meet. Frolo Dating is especially useful as it’s designed specifically for single parents. When preparing for a first date after a long absence from the dating scene, focus on self-presentation and setting the right environment to make the experience comfortable and enjoyable.

### **How do I balance dating with parenting?**

Prioritise your child’s needs while setting aside dedicated time for your dating life. Being upfront about your availability with potential partners can help manage expectations.

### **Should I mention my children in my dating profile?**

Yes, being honest from the start can help attract partners who respect and appreciate your role as a parent.

### **How do I handle co-parenting while dating?**

Maintain open communication with your co-parent and ensure any new relationship does not disrupt your child’s stability. Setting clear boundaries and respecting existing arrangements is crucial.

### **What if my date doesn’t understand my responsibilities as a parent?**

If someone struggles to accept your responsibilities as a parent, they may not be the right match. Look for someone who values and respects your priorities.

### **Is it normal to feel guilty about dating as a single parent?**

Yes, many single parents feel guilt when re-entering the dating scene. Remember, taking care of your happiness can positively impact your parenting.

### **How do I deal with rejection while dating as a single parent?**

Rejection is part of dating for everyone. If a match isn’t understanding of your situation, they aren’t the right fit for you. Keep a positive mindset and continue searching for someone who aligns with your values.

### **What are some red flags to watch out for when dating as a single parent?**

Be cautious of partners who:  
- Don’t respect your time and commitments.  
- Show little interest in your family life.  
- Want to rush the relationship too quickly.  
- Are secretive or dishonest about their own situation.

### **Can I find a serious relationship as a single parent?**

Of fourse! Many single parents find meaningful, lasting relationships – I'm testament to that for sure. The key is to be open, patient, and to use the right platforms to meet like-minded individuals.

### **What are the benefits of dating another single parent?**

Dating another single parent often leads to a deeper understanding of priorities, greater flexibility, and a mutual appreciation of parenting challenges and joys.

### **How do I navigate dating apps as a single parent?**

Be clear in your profile about your parental status and expectations. Choose apps that cater to single parents, such as Frolo Dating, and filter potential matches based on shared values.

### **How do I introduce my new partner to my child?**

Take it slow and ensure the relationship is stable before making introductions. Choose a neutral setting, keep the first meeting casual, and be open to your child’s feelings and reactions.

### **Should I date someone who doesn’t have children?**

It depends on their understanding and willingness to embrace your parental responsibilities. Some people without kids are great partners for single parents, while others may struggle with the dynamics of parenting.
