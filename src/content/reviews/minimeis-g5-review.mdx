## Introduction to the MiniMeis Shoulder Carrier

If you're on the lookout for a hands-free way to carry your child while keeping them engaged with the world around them, the MiniMeis shoulder carrier could be exactly what you need.

The MiniMeis G5 is the latest update in the brand’s line-up, known for being the lightest framed child carrier in its category—folding down neatly to the size of a backpack.

But what is it?

Well, this Scandinavian invention has quickly become one of the best creations for modern parenting, offering a clever and lightweight design that lets your child ride safely and comfortably on your shoulders. Designed for kids aged six months to five years old, it's ideal for everything from family day trips to countryside hikes.

## Choosing the MiniMeis G5 shoulder carrier

Ever since finding out we were going to have another baby last year, I knew a MiniMeis review would be on the cards, since I couldn't afford a shoulder carrier for Evie, my eight-year-old, when she was little (and riding on her parent's shoulders has been one of her preferred methods of transport for as long as I can remember!).

Now Millie is old enough to use one, we thought it was time we purchased one and put it to the test!

## First Impressions and Unboxing

When our MiniMeis G5 arrived, we were impressed by how sturdy and compact the kit felt even before opening it. It comes folded flat in a sleek package with all the essential accessories—including ankle straps, a waist strap, and a full set of shoulder straps that can be easily adjusted.

Setup was intuitive, though the included user guide is useful—especially if you’re a first-time user.

We first tested the G5 during our visit to West Green House Gardens in Hampshire and it didn’t disappoint. Millie, our six-month-old, absolutely loved the ride.

The only drawback? She kept pulling my hair so hard I thought it might come out—but that’s more of a baby behaviour issue than a flaw in the carrier!

## Key Features and Benefits of the MiniMeis Carrier

The MiniMeis G5 carrier is packed with features that make it a top pick for adventurous parents:

- Padded seat and full backrest for comfortably supporting your child
- Secure leg straps and ankle straps to keep your toddler stable
- A waist belt and waist strap that help distribute weight more evenly across your body, avoiding direct pressure on your neck and shoulders, and helping you maintain your balance
- Shoulder straps that are padded and adjustable for a perfect fit
- Durable, easy-to-clean materials that are perfect for various outdoor trips
- Only 1kg in weight, making it the lightest framed child carrier around

This isn’t just a child carrier—it’s a smart piece of parenting tech that feels like one of those inventions you wish you’d found sooner.

## Comfort and Ergonomics: Designed for Both Parent and Child

![](/images/wp-uploads/2025/05/minimeis-g5-carrier-back-1024x819.jpg)

Comfort is key with any baby carrier, and the MiniMeis G5 delivers on all fronts.

For the parent, the carrier's body is designed to sit securely on your shoulders, with a padded waist belt. This helped to ensure Millie's weight was evenly distributed which, since she's still just a baby, really helped provide a sense of balance throughout our little adventure. This reduces strain on your neck and back—especially important when you're walking for long periods.

It's clear the designers thought long and hard about this aspect of the G5 when they tested their product, as this distribution of load will definitely pay dividends as Millie becomes an older child and starts to gain more weight.

For your child, the padded seat, backrest, and safety straps ensure a secure, upright position. Millie sat comfortably for over an hour on her shoulder-top throne, taking in the scenery (and occasionally yanking my hair again for a laugh).

The breathable design also keeps both you and your child cool in hot weather (it was 25 degrees Celsius on this particular day), making this carrier ideal for warm-day hikes, park visits, or summer festivals.

## Is the MiniMeis G5 Suitable for Your Child?

![](/images/wp-uploads/2025/05/3ce5523b-8a9d-4c3f-a522-02a103c22d04-819x1024.jpg)

The MiniMeis G5 is suitable for little ones from six months—as soon as they can sit up without support—up to about five years or 22kg.

For families with an older child who no longer fits in a traditional sling but still tires easily on long walks, the G5 offers the perfect solution.

It’s also ideal if you have a pair of children and only want to take one pushchair with you—the older one gets a top-deck seat, while the younger rides below.

The new G5 collection now comes in a matte premium fabric, which is made from recycled ocean waste materials.

The shoulder straps now have extra padding, which makes them softer for those longer journeys.

There's now velcro straps and no adjustments on the back, which makes it easier to use.

MiniMeis claim to have refined the detailing by colour-matching components, improving upon the stitch work, and molding the logos.

## Comparison: MiniMeis Carrier vs Traditional Carriers

Unlike traditional carriers that strap your baby to your chest or back, the MiniMeis shoulder carrier places your child high on your shoulders—offering them a whole new view of the world.

Something I didn't expect when it came to Millie taking pride of place on my shoulders was just how much attention she'd get up there. Lots of people stopped to talk to her, with one woman muttering under her breath within earshot, "Argh, that baby is so cute!".

I enjoyed that.

Traditional carriers often focus on closeness and sleep support. In contrast, the G5 is all about adventures, fun, and letting your child explore from a safe, elevated position.

### Durability and Quality

The MiniMeis carrier is built to last. Whether you're walking, hiking, or running errands, it holds up to the challenge.

Everything about it—from the zips to the straps and the carrier’s body—feels solid. It’s clearly designed to withstand trips, spills, and years of use.

The materials are water-resistant and easy to wipe clean, which is a lifesaver when you’re dealing with snacks, dribble, and muddy shoes.

### What Do Other Parents Say?

Online reviews are glowing, with most parents praising its compact design, comfort, and the sheer fun of letting their kids ride on their shoulders without the usual strain.

Many mention how excited their little ones are to be up high—laughing, pointing, and waving at the world around them.

The company’s excellent customer service and helpful instruction video also get a big thumbs-up, especially from first-time users.

### Is the MiniMeis G5 Carrier Worth It?

Ultimately, if you're looking for a child carrier that combines style, safety, and practicality, the MiniMeis G5 shoulder carrier is absolutely worth considering.

It’s a great investment for parents who value adventure, flexibility, and keeping their kids engaged on the go. Because of its compact design, you'll be able to easily stick a backpack on while you carry your baby and, considering you'll be able to get the best part of five years' use out of it, it does start to feel like value for money.

While it may not replace your full-time pushchair (note: your kids definitely wouldn't want to fall asleep in one of these things), it’s one of the smartest essentials to have for short trips, nature walks, or exploring busy areas where wheels get in the way.
