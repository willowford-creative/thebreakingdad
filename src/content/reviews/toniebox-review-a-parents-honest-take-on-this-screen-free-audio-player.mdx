## How Tonies Content is Stored Digitally

An interesting aspect of the Toniebox system is how content is **stored digitally**. When you place a Tonie on **top of the Toniebox**, it doesn't actually contain the audio—instead, it triggers the download of content from the cloud through your **internet connection**. After that initial download, the content remains on your Toniebox, allowing for **screen free** enjoyment without constant connectivity.
