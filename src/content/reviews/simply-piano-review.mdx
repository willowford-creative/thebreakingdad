Are you keen to learn piano but unsure where to start? In this comprehensive Simply Piano review, we'll explore whether this popular piano app can truly help you master the keyboard and develop proper piano technique.

## What Is Simply Piano?

Simply Piano is a piano learning app developed by JoyTunes that aims to teach absolute beginners how to play the piano through interactive lessons and real-time feedback. The app has gained tremendous popularity, with millions of downloads on both the App Store and Google Play.

[![](/images/wp-uploads/2025/05/220609_Product_02-1-1024x536.jpeg)](https://simplypiano.sjv.io/c/2715260/1375946/16489)

## How Does Simply Piano Work?

The Simply Piano app uses your device's microphone to detect the notes you play, providing instant feedback on whether you're hitting the right note or playing wrong notes. This interactive approach helps users learn piano basics and develop their skills at their own pace.

The app offers structured lessons that gradually introduce new concepts, from reading sheet music to playing chords and more complex compositions. Each lesson includes practice mode options that allow you to master skills before moving forward.

I had piano lessons for a few years as a teenager and, because of our financial situation, we couldn't afford private lessons. Simply Piano works at **your own pace**, which means you can develop as slowly or as quickly as **you need**.

## Sheet Music

Simply Piano excels at teaching users how to read sheet music effectively. Unlike traditional methods that can feel overwhelming, the app introduces notation gradually with clear visual cues that highlight the relationship between sheet music and the keyboard. The interactive format allows you to see notes light up on the on-screen keyboard while simultaneously viewing them on the staff.

The app's sheet music interface is particularly beginner-friendly, using colour-coding and highlighting to help users make connections between written notes and their keyboard positions. As you progress, Simply Piano gradually removes these training wheels, transitioning you to reading standard sheet music with confidence.

For those who find traditional sheet music intimidating, Simply Piano offers a bridge that makes the process more approachable while still developing legitimate reading skills that transfer to any printed music you might encounter outside the app.

## Piano Basics

Simply Piano structures its curriculum around solid piano basics that establish proper fundamentals. The app begins with keyboard orientation, helping absolute beginners understand the layout of white and black keys, octaves, and basic fingering positions.

Early lessons focus on critical foundational elements such as:

- Hand positioning and basic posture
- Single-note melodies using both hands independently
- Gradual introduction of timing and rhythm concepts
- Simple exercises that build finger dexterity and coordination

These piano basics are presented in a sequential, logical order that mirrors traditional teaching methods while leveraging the app's technology to make learning more engaging. The gamified approach transforms what could be tedious technical exercises into enjoyable challenges that build essential muscle memory and keyboard familiarity.

## Simply Piano Features

### Comprehensive Learning Path

Simply Piano offers courses suitable for absolute beginners through to intermediate level pianists. The app starts with piano basics and progressively introduces more advanced concepts including:

- Reading sheet music (including treble clef notation)
- Playing chords and chord progressions
- Developing sight reading abilities
- Building hand coordination
- Understanding music theory fundamentals

### Extensive Song Library

One of the most appealing aspects of Simply Piano is its extensive library of songs across different musical tastes. Users can learn to play:

- Classical pieces
- Pop songs
- Film soundtracks
- Rock classics

Each song comes with a backing track that makes practice sessions more engaging and fun. The app also allows you to learn a few songs of your choice right from the beginning, which keeps motivation high.

### Piano Keyboard Options

Simply Piano works with:

- Acoustic pianos
- Digital keyboards
- MIDI keyboard connections
- On-screen keyboard (for practice when away from your instrument)

For optimal results, connecting a MIDI keyboard provides the most authentic experience and helps develop proper hand position and muscle memory. I like this option because I can use a MIDI connection on our electric piano in our living room paired with headphones. My six-month-old daughter **does not** enjoy listening to me practice (which tells you something about my ability...), so it's been a lifesaver.

Also, the responsiveness you get from a direct audio feed is fantastic!

## Instant Feedback

One of Simply Piano's most powerful educational features is its instant feedback system. Using your device's microphone, the app listens as you play and provides immediate visual cues about accuracy. This instant feedback loop dramatically accelerates the learning process compared to traditional practice where mistakes might go uncorrected.

The instant feedback comes in several forms:

- Visual indicators showing correct and incorrect notes
- Timing guidance that helps develop rhythmic precision
- Progression tracking that identifies patterns in your mistakes
- Achievement metrics that celebrate improvement

This constant stream of instant feedback creates a highly efficient learning environment that helps prevent the formation of bad habits. The app won't let you progress until you've mastered each concept, ensuring a solid foundation before building more complex skills.

Moreover, the positive reinforcement aspect of the instant feedback system helps maintain motivation, a crucial factor in successful piano learning. Rather than feeling discouraged by mistakes, users receive constructive guidance on how to improve.

## Limitations to Consider

Despite its strengths, there are some aspects where Simply Piano falls short:

- Less emphasis on proper hand position than with a traditional teacher
- Limited feedback on expression and musicality
- Some advanced techniques require more specialised instruction
- The structured path doesn't always accommodate different learning styles

## Is Simply Piano Worth It? Our Honest Opinion
