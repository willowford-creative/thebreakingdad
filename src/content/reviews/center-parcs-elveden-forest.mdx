Center Parcs has become synonymous with family holidays across the UK, and after experiencing Center Parcs Elveden Forest firsthand, I can understand why families return year after year to these woodland villages.

## Why We Chose Center Parcs Elveden Forest

After years of caravan holidays, we wanted to mix it up a little bit for our first trip with a baby, as well as taking a well-earned chance to bond as a [blended family](/separated-parents/what-is-a-blended-family/). Center Parcs came [highly recommended for families,](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Hotel_Review-g2470936-d573930-Reviews-Center_Parcs_Elveden_Forest-Elveden_Suffolk_East_Anglia_England.html) and Elveden Forest ticked all the boxes with child-friendly activities, baby facilities, and the famous Subtropical Swimming Paradise (Honestly, the swimming pool was the biggest draw for us).

Center Parcs UK operates several forest villages, including Sherwood Forest, Whinfell Forest, Woburn Forest, and Longleat Forest, but Elveden Forest's location (2.5 hours away), along with its reputation for young kids and baby-friendly facilities made it our top choice for our first Center Parcs holiday.

## Booking & Arrival

![Evie and I take a selfie together just after we arrive at Center Parcs Elveden Forest](/images/wp-uploads/2025/06/Facetune_18-06-2025-18-38-40-791x1024.jpg)

- Smooth, easy online booking with guest services support
- Helpful staff on arrival
- Car park access and lodge check-in were straightforward
- Free welcome pack information about all the forest activities

The booking process couldn't have been simpler, and the prices shown during off-peak periods offered excellent value compared to school holidays. As first-time visitors, the queue to get in with our car and the chaos of the car park seemed a little overwhelming to start with, but there's actually plenty of space if you patiently follow the one-way system.  
  
Everything was clearly signposted and we quickly found our way to where we needed to be.

## Family-Friendly Lodges at Elveden Forest

![Lodge 578 at Center Parcs Elveden Forest](/images/wp-uploads/2025/06/IMG_7683-1024x768.jpg)

We stayed in Lodge 538 (Maple area) — close to the centre, quiet, and surrounded by woodland. The family lodge was fully equipped with a cot, high chairs, and spacious living areas, perfect for travelling with a baby and older kids.

Our accommodation featured everything needed for a comfortable break: a well-appointed kitchen for preparing our own food, comfortable seating areas to relax, and bedrooms that provided peace after busy days exploring the forest.

We'd recommend bringing your own toys to keep the kids entertained. If you're not sure, check out the [best toys for 10 year olds](/parenting/best-toys-for-10-year-olds-2025-guide/) or best travel toys for toddlers for some handy tips!

**Wildlife highlight:** A deer came right up to our patio and fed from the girls' hands — a magical moment none of us will forget. This lovely little encounter with nature perfectly captures what makes Center Parcs villages so special, although the bold squirrel who ventured into our lodge trying to find scraps became something of an annoyance as the week progressed!

## Subtropical Swimming Paradise: The Highlight

This pool complex is worth the hype and stands as the crown jewel of any Center Parcs resort.

- Our baby loved the shallow water areas
- The older girls couldn't get enough of the slides and rapids
- Evie overcame her fear of the Cyclone slide — a proud parenting moment and one of her top memories
- The wild water rapids provided thrills for older children
- Outdoor pools complement the main complex during warmer months
- Plunge pool was great fun for the kids to 'challenge' each other

The tropical cyclone ride became a firm favourite, whilst the changing rooms were spacious and well-maintained throughout our stay.

One of my highlights of the week was taking Evie overcoming her fear of the Cyclone ride. On our first day, she went to the top, panicked and wanted to come back down again. After some words of encouragement, she ventured back up again a few days later and, though she was scared, she absolutely loved it.

**Tip:** Booking a cabana can help with naps, but we found it a bit pricey for what you get. We had a cabana for two days, the Bamboo room right in the middle of the pool. Though the drinks fridge was nice, the TV was hard to hear because of the background pool noise and we agreed we'd probably have been better off just getting there earlier and securing one of the more secluded seating areas instead.

## Aqua Sana Spa Experience

I booked Rosie into the Aqua Sana Spa on our first day for a full-body massage, which she thoroughly deserved.

Many guests rave about the forest bathing experiences and thermal pools that make Aqua Sana a destination in itself, which I can absolutely believe.

## Is Center Parcs Worth the Cost?

We've stayed at Parkdean and similar caravan parks in the past — and yes, Center Parcs is more expensive. But the quality, service, and overall experience were levels above.

#### **What We'd Skip Next Time:**

- Teddy Bear Making
- Cabanas (helpful for naps, but not essential)
- Arcade (great games, but overpriced at £12 for 10 tokens)

#### **What We'd Book Earlier:**

- Dinner at Huck's. It was fully booked during our visit
- First night restaurant reservations after all that travel.

To save money, consider bringing your own food for some meals and booking activities selectively based on your children's interests.

Whether you choose Elveden Forest or explore other Center Parcs parks like Sherwood Forest or Whinfell Forest, you're guaranteed a memorable family holiday in these magical woodland villages.

In fact, we'll definitely be back again next year if we can.

## Frequently Asked Questions:

### **Does Elveden Center Parcs have a lazy river?**

Yes, Center Parcs Elveden Forest features a lazy river as part of its Subtropical Swimming Paradise. It's a relaxing highlight for families, and children love floating through the lush indoor setting.

### **Is Center Parcs Elveden Forest good for babies?**

Yes, it's fantastic for babies. There are baby changing stations, baby sensory classes, shallow pool areas, Ella's Kitchen baby food stations, and high chairs and cots in the lodges. It's a very family- and baby-friendly environment.

### **How much is Center Parcs for a week with a family?**

Prices vary greatly depending on the season, size of your lodge, and how early you book. We visited during term time and saved significantly, but you can expect to pay more during school holidays. Check the prices shown during booking for the best deals, and consider that Center Parcs does offer discounts for repeat visitors.

### **Are restaurants at Center Parcs Elveden family-friendly?**

Absolutely. We especially enjoyed Las Iguanas for both the food and family-friendly service. They even had a robot waiter, which the kids loved. Other options include Huck's, the Pancake House, Bella Italia, and more — though it's wise to book early.
