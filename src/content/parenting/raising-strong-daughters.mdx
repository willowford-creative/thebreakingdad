It's no secret that I love being a Dad. I see raising strong daughters as more than a goal, it's my responsibility. You should too.

Since having a daughter, I've realised that us Dads can play a fundamental role in helping our little girls become the fierce women they deserve to be.

## Understanding Privilege

We’re surrounded by white male privilege. By my very race and gender, I’m a beneficiary. It doesn’t sit quite right with me.

Nothing could have possibly opened my eyes more than the birth of my baby girl. That moment marked a significant effect on my perspective and shaped how I approach raising daughters and understanding my role in my daughter's life.

I love my daughter more than life itself. Becoming a parent taught me the value of family.

More importantly, being there for my daughter has shown me the importance of raising a strong daughter in a world of imbalance. A father's love is crucial in nurturing her growth and emotional well-being.

Kids learn how to view the world from those closest to them. In their early years, it is predominantly from their parents. Fathers play a vital role in shaping their daughters’ lives by teaching them self-respect and self-reliance.

## How to Raise a Daughter as a Father

Here's a simple guide on how to raise a daughter as a father.

It's by no means extensive, but it should start you off.

## Why Should you be Motivated to Raise a Strong Daughter?

![A daddy and his daughter ](/images/wp-uploads/2020/12/IMG_4642-2-scaled.jpg)

For me, this is all about helping you to support your daughter to be strong, just as it's my motivation for supporting my own little girl.

I’ve been surrounded by tough women my whole life. They taught me how to be respectful as a man, as well as how to raise a strong girl.

One of my favourite compliments to give Evie is this:

> “You’re so kind.”

Kindness is currency.

A warm heart is a vital foundation in the making of a good parent, an effective employee and one strong little girl.

Of course, kindness can be taken for granted.

Sometimes you need to take a leap of faith in order to share a happy and fulfilling life.

To raise a girl who is happy in herself and to support her in being whatever she wants to be is the ultimate opportunity.

## How to Raise a Self-Confident Daughter

As a father, I don't want my daughter to be defined by superficial values. I want her to feel that she has unlimited potential, the capability to do whatever she so chooses in this life.

My role isn't to patronise or carry her, it's to support her in galvanising that sense of self belief.

### Combatting Social Pressure

Women are under more pressure than ever to look good, perhaps even more so than men.

Social media amplifies this obsession even more and creates a kind of ‘en mass peer pressure’ that of ideals they feel they need to abide by. I want my little girl to know there’s more to life than looks.

Meaningful conversations are crucial in combatting these social pressures. Engaging in deep discussions helps build emotional security and trust, fostering healthy relationships and personal growth.

Kindness, decency, resilience, and courage should be the yardstick of success when raising a strong girl. Fathers must be actively involved in building their daughters’ confidence and encouraging their emotional support. Sometimes I think we, as dads, underestimate the power we have in lifting our little girls up.

### Compliment Traits That Matter for Self Worth

Compliment your daughter on traits that really matter:

- Resilience
- Courage
- Intelligence

### Be a Strong Male Figure in Her Life

As a Dad, if you ever find yourself asking how to raise a strong daughter, there's a simple answer: Lead by example.

#### Love Her Mother to Model a Healthy Relationship

I separated with Evie’s mum back in 2016. Since then, we’ve had our fair share of ups and downs.

One thing I’ve NEVER done is criticise my ex in front of Evie.

A little girl needs to learn from an early age that a man should treat a woman with respect, as it will influence the type of partner she'll fall in love with in the future.

#### Foster Attachment

Don't underestimate how important it is for your daughter to form an attachment to you.

Spend quality time with them, play games, and let them know you love them often with words as well as physical affection.

#### Celebrate Her Mind

Be honestly enthusiastic and interested in the things she tells you and take time to understand the way she thinks.

## Fostering Independence and Self-Reliance

![A dad has his arm around his daughter, holding her supportively as they walk together](/images/wp-uploads/2020/12/IMG_8076.jpg)

The fastest way to teach a child independence is to let them try things and sometimes fail at them. Easier said than done as a parent who just wants to step in and fix it.

I try to give Evie space to make her own choices, even when they're small. Which jumper to wear. Which book to read first. Whether to climb the bigger frame at the park (within reason). Each tiny decision is a rep, and she learns that she's the one driving, not me.

Modelling matters too. If she sees me asking for help only when I genuinely need it, owning my mistakes, and making decisions without anxiously checking what other people think, she'll do the same. The opposite is also true.

## Highlighting and Acknowledging Sexism

There are so many confusing double-standards in today's society.

For example, assertive men are considered favourable, but assertive women are typically dismissed as rude or abrasive.

### Challenging Gender Norms and Gender Stereotypes

![A comic strip highlighting the double-standard of men and women in a range of situations](/images/wp-uploads/2020/12/Double-standards-1-1-1.jpg)

It's not hard to spot how dominated by male figures most children's literature is, particularly in fairytales. That alone reinforces gender stereotypes.

More concerning is how often the girls are portrayed as acted upon rather than active. Objects or supporting roles, basically… Not aspirational role models.

## You've Got a Battle on Your Hands

If you really want to know how to raise a good daughter, you need to encourage them to be themselves, right into their teen years and beyond.

A good man should know how to teach girls that they can be the person they want to be unapologetically, unconditionally, and unwaveringly.

If we ever hope to bring genuine gender equality, then men need to take action too.

### Final Thoughts

If you get it right, she'll challenge the rules, particularly during the teen years… so I'm told. Perfect.

Your little girl needs to understand that her opinion matters. She needs to know her voice is heard.

You definitely won't always agree with her.

Be patient, it's a sacrifice you're going to have to make.

What do you get in return?

You'll have guided your little lady through her life, imbued with the sense of self-worth that she deserves.

Want your daughter to take a leap of faith and be who she wants to be? You need to be there to catch her. No matter what.

That's what I plan to do with Evie.
