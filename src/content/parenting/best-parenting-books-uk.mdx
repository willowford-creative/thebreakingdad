## What Makes a Great Parenting Book?

Not all parenting books are created equal. The best ones for grown-ups raising kids are:

- **Science-based:** Grounded in research from psychology, neuroscience, or child development.
- **Practical:** Full of tips you can actually use in daily life with young kids.
- **Relatable:** Written with warmth, humour, and empathy for the parenting process.
- **Culturally relevant:** Applicable to life as a parent in the UK, considering our healthcare system, schooling, and societal norms.

If you're searching for trusted advice on parenting that actually works in the real world, here's what to look for — and exactly where to start your reading journey.
