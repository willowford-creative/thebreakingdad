Planning a family day out in Hampshire? No worries...There's plenty to do. From thrilling theme parks to educational museums, there's something for everyone. Here's a curated list of top attractions to enjoy with your kids.

## **1. Paultons Park & Peppa Pig World**

![Evie poses on gaston at Peppa Pig World](/images/wp-uploads/2025/02/IMG_7286-768x1024.jpg)

Paultons Park is one of the **best family attractions in the UK**, with **over 70 rides** set in a beautiful theme park. Younger kids will love **Peppa Pig World**, while older children can enjoy roller coasters like **Storm Chaser** and **The Flight of the Pterosaur**. There’s also a **dinosaur-themed Lost Kingdom**, splash parks, and lovely gardens to explore.

- **Address**: Ower, Romsey SO51 6AL
- **Directions**: Located off the M27, exit at junction 2. [Get directions](https://goo.gl/maps/WcPnmtxC9TQ2)
- **Website**: [Paultons Park](https://paultonspark.co.uk)
- **TripAdvisor Reviews**: Read reviews

## 2. Marwell Zoo

![ On the train at Marwell Zoo](/images/wp-uploads/2025/02/IMG_0301-768x1024.jpg)

Explore a diverse range of animals in a sprawling 140-acre park. Marwell Zoo is dedicated to conservation and offers a great day out for animal lovers.

- **Address**: Thompson's Lane, Colden Common, Winchester SO21 1JH
- **Directions**: Located off the B2177 between Winchester and Bishops Waltham. [Get directions](https://goo.gl/maps/HuNns35kKcH2)
- **Website**: [Marwell Zoo](https://www.marwell.org.uk)
- **TripAdvisor Reviews**: [Read reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g186300-d208919-Reviews-Marwell_Zoo-Winchester_Hampshire_England.html)

![](/images/wp-uploads/2025/02/IMG_4035-1024x768.jpg)

Kids can watch the **trains being prepared**, take a turn in the driver’s seat on special event days, and enjoy the **model railway exhibition**.

- **Address**: Wide Lane, Eastleigh SO50 5PE
- **Directions**: Located near Eastleigh, just off the M3. Get directions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best theme park for kids in Hampshire?

Paultons Park, home to Peppa Pig World, is widely considered the best theme park for young children, offering a mix of gentle rides, themed attractions, and thrilling roller coasters for older kids.

### Are there free things to do with kids in Hampshire?

Yes! The New Forest Reptile Centre, Bolderwood Deer Sanctuary, and many country parks such as Queen Elizabeth Country Park offer fantastic free activities for families.

### What are the best indoor activities for kids in Hampshire?

For rainy days, consider visiting Winchester Science Centre, The Brickworks Museum, or Funland Hayling Island for indoor fun and learning experiences.

### Where can I take my kids to see animals in Hampshire?

Marwell Zoo, Longdown Activity Farm, and the New Forest Wildlife Park are all excellent choices for seeing and interacting with animals.

### What are some good outdoor activities for families in Hampshire?

Alice Holt Forest, Moors Valley Country Park, and Staunton Country Park all offer great opportunities for walking, cycling, and outdoor play areas.
