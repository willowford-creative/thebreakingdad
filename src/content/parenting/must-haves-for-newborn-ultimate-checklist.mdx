## Creating a Baby Home That Works for You: Baby's Nursery Baby Essentials

Your baby's nursery doesn't need to be Pinterest-perfect. A safe, simple baby home setup works just fine with these newborn essentials:

- Cot or moses basket with a quality cot mattress
- Waterproof mattress cover (essential for accidents)
- Clothes storage and a changing table
- Rocking chair or glider for night feeds
- Baby monitor for peace of mind (We chose the [BOIFUN 2K Wifi Video Baby Monitor](https://amzn.to/44c4ZVD))
- Soft play mat for tummy time
- Portable cot for flexible sleeping arrangements

Think about your baby's small size and changing needs over the first few weeks. Consider whether you'll transition to a toddler bed later and plan accordingly. A night light can be helpful for monitoring your baby's temperature and comfort during feeds.
