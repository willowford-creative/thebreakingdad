If you're looking for a scenic, family-friendly day out in Hampshire, **West Green House Gardens** is a must-visit destination. Set just outside the village of **Hartley Wintney**, this beautifully landscaped garden with a rich 300-year history is open from spring through to Christmas, offering seasonal delights and peaceful strolls year-round.

As National Trust members, Rosie and I have visited lots of sites across the country, and oddly, the West Green House gardens are the closest to us, but we've never been.

Isn't that always the way?!

Last weekend, Rosie, baby Millie and I were blown away by the stunning feature walks, along with the warm welcome from the staff, and the tranquil setting.

Whether you're into horticulture (I can't say I am), history, or just a relaxing cream tea, there's something special waiting behind the garden gates.

## Arrival

West Green House is easy to find using sat nav, with plenty of space to park your car. A short walk past the greenhouse and you'll find the gift shop, which also doubles as garden entry.

We had a warm and friendly welcome, with clear instructions and guidance as to how to explore the gardens for our experience at the West Green House gardens.

## Garden Features

![](/images/wp-uploads/2025/05/download.jpeg)

From formal borders to playful follies (ornamental buildings), West Green House Gardens offers a rich variety of landscaping features, starting in the wild gardens and moving through to the more manicured formal gardens:

- **Ten ornamental follies**, including a stunning Nymphaeum that overlooks the historic Walled Garden
- **Paradise Garden**, featuring a beautiful central fountain and the **Five Bridges Garden**, which is stunning this time of year
- **Spring blooms** include tulips, daffodils, and hyacinths that transform the gardens into a tapestry of colour
- **Carefully designed pathways** make for an enchanting and enjoyable walk, particularly for kids and curious explorers alike

The whole site has been designed and nurtured by **Marylyn Abbott**, whose personal touch gives the gardens both structure and soul.

## The Green House

![Rosie and Millie in the West Green House greenhouse](/images/wp-uploads/2025/05/IMG_6427-1024x768.jpg)

The estate’s **18th-century country house** is listed as **Grade II*** and stands as the centrepiece of the grounds. Though privately managed, the house and gardens rival any National Trust property in terms of beauty and historical value.

We absolutely loved the wisteria that frames the entrance to the greenhouse – perfect for a quick photo!

## Events and Activities

West Green House Gardens offers more than just a beautiful view. Each summer, the gardens host a **popular opera season**, where guests can enjoy live performances beside the lake, surrounded by blooming borders and candlelit pathways.  
  
Other events include:

- 🎄 **Christmas celebrations** including festive lighting and seasonal displays
- ☕ **Tea room**, great for teas and coffees or light lunches—**the sausage rolls were top-notch**, though slightly on the pricey side
- 🎁 **Gift Shop** with plants, books, and artisan gifts
- 👧 **Children’s trails and activities**, making this a great spot for families

## Conservation Efforts

This garden is as much about sustainability as it is about beauty. Conservation efforts include:

- Wildlife-friendly planting that attracts birds, bees and butterflies
- Organic gardening principles where possible
- Ongoing restoration of heritage features like the Walled Garden and bridges

As a visitor, it's a joy to explore a space that’s both carefully preserved and forward-thinking.

## Partnership with National Trust

Although **not owned or managed by the National Trust**, West Green House Gardens is featured in their garden partnership scheme. This means **National Trust members enjoy free entry** upon showing a National Trust Membership card, helping to make it even more accessible to heritage enthusiasts. Keep in mind though, that this doesn't apply to special events. If you head along to any of those, you'll need a separate ticket.

The gardens represent a key part of **Hampshire’s cultural and horticultural heritage**, attracting thousands of visitors every year.

## Visitor Information

- 📅 **Open Wednesday–Sunday, March 12 to November 2 in 2025**, including Bank Holidays
- 🕐 Hours: 11:00 AM – 4:30 PM (The Tea Room is open 11:00 AM – 4 PM)
- 🎟️ Entry: £10 adults, £5 children (3–14), under 3s go free
- 🍽️ Tearoom & Gift Shop onsite
- 💻 [Book tickets and view opening times](https://www.westgreenhouse.co.uk)

Facilities are family-friendly, and paths are largely accessible, although some gravel paths and bridges may be trickier with pushchairs or mobility aids. You don't need to pre-book tickets, although again, for special events this may be a requirement.

## Reviews and Testimonials

With a **TripAdvisor rating of 4.5 stars**, West Green House Gardens is widely praised for:

## Verdict: Worth the visit?

Whether you’re planning a romantic day out, looking for weekend family fun, or simply want a quiet spot to reconnect with nature, **West Green House Gardens in Hampshire** is a hidden gem. With its unique blend of artistry, nature, and cultural events, it offers something truly memorable in every season. We absolutely loved our visit and will definitely be going back with the rest of our kids very soon!
